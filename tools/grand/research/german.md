# German AOK 10, 9 September – 31 December 1943 (grand campaign research)

Companion to `german.json` (units with dated positions, lines with points, events, losses, strengths). Every statement
below carries its source. Coordinates in the JSON come from the Wikipedia article of each town (URL kept per position);
where no article or no coordinates existed, a nearby comune is used as a proxy and the entry says so.

## Command

- AOK 10: von Vietinghoff; Hube acting 20–28 Sep while Vietinghoff stood in for Rommel (Molony V p.334 and fn); Lemelsen
  from 5 Nov to 28 Dec after Vietinghoff went sick (Molony V p.448; Blumenson p.224). Fighting troops on 1 Oct: 60,443
  (Molony V p.432 fn); "about 60,000" assigned, of which about 35,000 in XIV Pz Corps facing Fifth Army (Blumenson p.190).
- XIV Pz Corps: Hube, Balck acting 2 Sep–end Oct, Hube again early Oct, von Senger from 28 Oct (Molony V p.267 fn, 432 fn, 448).
- LXXVI Pz Corps: Herr throughout (Molony V p.291, 457).
- Army Group C / OB Südwest: Kesselring took up full command on 21 Nov (Molony V p.379, 477).
- Corps boundary 18 Sep: Mt Stella (Pt 951, SE of Baronissi) – Arienzo – Venafro (Molony V p.339). After that LXXVI Pz
  Corps held everything east of the Matese/Apennine spine.

## 1. Salerno, 9–18 September

Eve of the landing (Molony V p.267): XIV Pz Corps had 15 PG Div on the Gulf of Gaeta, HG Div around Naples, 16 Pz Div on
the Gulf of Salerno; 3 PG and 2 FJ Divs were near Rome under Fliegerkorps XI. Strengths on 31 Aug: 16 Pz 15,000, 15 PG
12,300, HG 15,700. 16 Pz had fit on 8 Sep: 7 Pz III, 12 Pz 38, 87 Pz IV; 37 assault guns; 16 Art Regt 18 SP + 20 towed
light howitzers and 4 10-cm guns (p.267–268).

16 Pz Div battle groups on 9 Sep, 3–6 miles inland (Molony V p.268 and fn):

| group | where | units |
|---|---|---|
| Dörnemann | Salerno–Baronissi | 16 Recce Bn, a company of III/2 Pz (assault guns), SP artillery, engineers |
| Stempel | Pontecagnano–Montecorvino–Battipaglia | 64 PG Regt (2 bns), III/2 Pz (less a company), a battery |
| von Holtey (reserve) | Persano | II/2 Pz less two companies (I/2 Pz was in Germany re-equipping with Panthers and never reached Italy) |
| von Doering | Albanella–Rutino | 79 PG Regt (2 bns), two companies II/2 Pz, a battery |

Elsewhere on 9 Sep (Molony V p.243 fn): HQ 1 FJ Div, 1 FJ Regt less a bn, and a bn of 4 FJ Regt in Apulia (based on
Altamura, rearguards to Castellaneta, Gioia del Colle, Ginosa, p.243); 3 and 4 FJ Regts (less detachments) in Calabria
under 26 Pz Div; III/1 FJ with HG near Naples. 26 Pz and 29 PG were withdrawing from Calabria and had swapped recce
battalions; 26 Pz had I and III/4 FJ attached and no tanks until 16 Sep (one tank bn not in Italy, the other with 3 PG
near Rome) (Molony V p.293 fn).

Also on 10 Sep: I/3 FJ (detached to 29 PG) went straight into Battipaglia under 16 Pz (Molony V p.291 and fn); II/1 HG
was at Cava and HG Recce Bn at the Chiunzi pass (p.292). LXXVI Pz Corps took over south of a Mt Giovi–Giffoni line
from noon 11 Sep, taking 16 Pz under command (p.291).

Main attack 13 Sep on the Sele–Calore gap: 16 Pz Stempel towards Bellizzi, von Doering down the railway to S. Nicola
Varco, group Kleine Limberg towards Persano; 29 PG with 71 PG, two companies 26 Recce and assault guns down the track to
Persano; 15 PG Regt held Altavilla (Molony V p.308). On 14–15 Sep 16 Pz and 29 PG went over to the defensive, 29 PG on
Persano–Altavilla–Controne (p.316). Schmalz (HG) attacked east of Salerno 14–15 Sep with I and II/129 (infantry) and
the HG Pz Regt + III/129 (armour), II/29 PG in one of them; HG Pz Regt on 15 Sep had 11 Pz III, 28 Pz IV, 2 Pz 38 and 49
assault guns; 2nd and 3rd batteries of 71 Werfer Regt supported (p.316 fn). 26 Pz's von Mandelsloh group (two bns 9 PG,
one bn 4 FJ) attacked towards Bellizzi and Fosso on 16 Sep and failed (p.321). II/1 FJ and II and III/3 FJ set off from
Apulia on 15 Sep but do not appear to have fought at Salerno; on 17 Sep the FJ battalions were to reassemble between
Potenza and Altamura (p.319 fn); Heidrich reported three battalions en route on 16 Sep (p.324).

Losses: German 840 killed, 2,002 wounded, 630 missing (3,472); 70 tanks and 21 assault guns lost (Molony V p.325).
Blumenson: HG about 1,000, 16 Pz about 1,300 casualties; 26 Pz, with one regiment two days in action, little affected
(Blumenson p.144).

## 2. Withdrawal, 17 September – 6 October

- Kesselring called the battle off on 17 Sep; withdrawal orders 18 Sep (Molony V p.324). 29 PG began to disengage 17 Sep,
  26 Pz about 19 Sep, both to LXXVI Pz Corps in the east; HG held the pivot with units of 3 PG, 15 PG and two FJ bns; 16 Pz
  was assembled and sent to build defences on the Volturno near Capua (Blumenson p.156).
- 18 Sep: Balck's line Castellammare – Corbara – S of Nocera – Cava – Mt Stella. 15 PG on the right at Castellammare; HG
  centre and left with II/67 PG, III/1 FJ and 16 Recce Bn under command (16 Recce back to XIV Pz Corps 19 Sep, to
  positions N and SW of Caserta by 22 Sep). 3 PG elements gathered into Battle Group Möller near Minturno with two bns
  of 29 PG Regt (Molony V p.339). 26 Pz went via Acerno to Avellino–Montemarano, 29 PG via Contursi to Teora (p.339).
- Infantry fighting strengths and frontages c.24 Sep: HG 4,691 men on 55 km; 26 Pz 3,232 on 34 km; 29 PG 2,225 on 44 km;
  1 FJ 2,000 (Molony V p.341 fn). About 8,000 men of 1 FJ faced Eighth Army towards Foggia (Blumenson p.155).
- Line O Salerno–Bovino–Manfredonia to be held to 30 Sep; then Line A (Volturno) and A.1 (Biferno), later named Viktor,
  to be held until at least 15 Oct (Molony V p.334). 14 Pz Corps' first intermediate position "Anton" ran Torre del
  Greco – Monteforte, occupied from the night 27/28 Sep (p.341–342). No troops south of Naples after 30 Sep/1 Oct (p.342).
- 1 FJ: line Potenza–Altamura (p.344); took positions W and N of Foggia on the night 26/27 Sep; Foggia fell 27 Sep (p.345).
- 90 PG: evacuated from Sardinia to Corsica by 19 Sep (with a fortress brigade, 25,800 men), Corsica evacuated by 3 Oct;
  Sturmbrigade Reichsführer SS was in Corsica too (Molony V p.375–376). Wikipedia: then near Pisa and at Gatteo a Mare on
  the Adriatic, Abruzzo from mid-November.

## 3. Termoli, 2–6 October

- LXXVI Pz Corps front from the Adriatic to the mountains held only by 1 FJ, mostly astride Highway 17 facing the
  Canadians; Schulz group (1 FJ Regt) at Larino with II/1 FJ about five miles from Termoli; the "nondescript" Rau group,
  400 strong, in Termoli (Molony V p.434). Wikipedia: Kampfgruppe Rau = an FJ engineer platoon with flak and transport
  troops, 383–600 men (Battle of Termoli).
- Parts of 16 Pz ordered east evening 1 Oct; Kesselring ordered the whole division to Campobasso on 2 Oct (Molony V
  p.434). Blumenson has Vietinghoff stalling and the move starting on the morning of 4 Oct, a 75-mile march, some
  elements at Termoli late that morning, the bulk 24 hours later (Blumenson p.190–191). Wikipedia (Volturno Line) gives 1
  Oct for Kesselring's order. Treat the start date as 1–4 Oct.
- 16 Pz groups at Termoli (Molony V p.434 fn, 435–436): Stenckhoff (2 Pz Regt, II/16 Art, engineers), von Doering (79 PG,
  2 bns, III/16 Art), Stempel (I/64 PG, I/16 Art; II/64 to follow); 16 Recce Bn reached Campobasso first. 4 Oct Stempel at
  Petacciato, von Doering at S. Giacomo; 5 Oct three companies of 2 Pz Regt with about 20 Pz IV. Herr sanctioned
  withdrawal 6 Oct 16:35. 16 Pz casualties 573 (p.436 and fn). LXXVI Pz Corps then withdrew to the Trigno (Blumenson p.191).
- 3 PG replaced 16 Pz on the Volturno, arriving in groups from about 10 Oct (Blumenson p.191).

## 4. Volturno and the approach to the winter positions, 7 October – 5 November

West (XIV Pz Corps):
- 11 Oct layout (Molony V p.438 and the rotated diagram on p.442; Blumenson p.190): 15 PG (Baade acting) coast to Brezza,
  east of Grazzanise, one regiment in line, the rest guarding the coast to the Garigliano; HG (Conrath) Brezza to Piana
  di Caiazzo with four infantry bns, a small armoured group and many assault and AA guns; 3 PG (Gräser) Caiazzo to
  Amorosi/Monte Acero; Battle Group Viebig of 26 Pz (incl. II/9 PG and the 26 Pz recce bn) on Monte Acero on the corps'
  left. The p.442 diagram is printed sideways and only partly legible in the OCR, so battalion details from it are marked
  uncertain in the JSON.
- After the crossings of 13 Oct, Hube pulled back step by step from late 14 Oct to the line Raviscanina – Mondragone by
  24 Oct (Molony V p.444–445). 26 Pz left for the Eighth Army front two days after 13 Oct (CMH 100-8 p.83 fn). Elements of
  8 PG Regt (3 PG) held Hill 235 near Pratella 26–27 Oct (CMH 100-8 p.76–77).
- Barbara line: Mondragone – Pietravairano – Colli a Volturno – Sessano, then the Trigno to S. Salvo (Molony V p.429);
  Blumenson: Monte Massico – Teano – Presenzano – Matese (p.207–208). Light field works for short rearguard action.
- 305 ID assembled near Cassino and 65 ID near Pescara by mid-Oct (Molony V p.432 fn); 94 ID also joined XIV Pz Corps in
  October (p.448). 14 Pz Corps lost over 3,500 men (914 sick) in the last three weeks of October; its three divisions
  averaged about 2,100 front-line infantry in mid-month (p.447). 11 Oct–10 Nov: 6,500 incl. 1,886 sick (p.454).

East (LXXVI Pz Corps):
- 1 FJ held Highway 17 against the Canadians: Heilmann group (3 FJ Regt plus a bn of 1 FJ), then II/67 PG, later 4 FJ
  Regt and part of 29 PG (Molony V p.437). Canadians took Gambatesa 8 Oct, Jelsi/Gildone 11 Oct, Campobasso and Vinchiaturo
  14–15 Oct unopposed (p.437).
- Vietinghoff sanctioned withdrawal to the Barbara line on 11 Oct: S. Salvo – Lentella – Celenza – Colli a Volturno,
  with depth on Vasto – Cupello – Furci – Carunchio – Forlì (Molony V p.457). 65 ID's trains were bombed near Pescara
  on 11 Oct (p.465).
- Divisions under LXXVI Pz Corps in October: 16 Pz, 26 Pz (to XIV from about 3 Nov), 29 PG (to XIV from about 31 Oct), 65
  ID (arriving), 1 FJ (Molony V p.456 fn). Casualties in October 2,884 (p.457 fn).
- Layout at the beginning of November (Molony V p.457): 16 Pz nearest the coast on the axis S. Salvo – Cupello – Scerni;
  1 FJ in the centre between Tufillo and Castiglione (3 FJ Regt at Tufillo); 26 Pz withdrawing on Vinchiaturo – Isernia;
  65 ID in the coastal sector of the Bernhardt line with parts forward on the Advanced Sangro line, Casoli – Fossacesia.
- Trigno, 27 Oct – 5 Nov: 64 PG held S. Salvo against 78 Div on 27/28 Oct (p.458); 3 FJ held Tufillo 2–4 Nov and gave it up
  on the 5th as 1 FJ stretched to replace 26 Pz (p.459–460); 3 Nov S. Salvo defended by 64 PG's two bns with II/2 Pz in
  reserve, 79 PG behind the Treste on the west flank, the III/2 Pz assault guns watching the coast north of Vasto; that
  evening Sieckenius began the planned withdrawal via Scerni and Atessa (p.460–461). 78 Div reached the Sangro by 9 Nov
  (p.461).
- 4 Nov fighting strengths (Molony V p.457 fn): 16 Pz 4,598; 1 FJ 4,424; 26 Pz printed "53390" in the OCR (probably
  5,390; check the printed book); 65 ID 6,552. Fit vehicles: 16 Pz 6 Pz IV, 5 command tanks, 9 assault guns; 26 Pz 28 Pz
  III, 42 Pz IV, 8 command, 12 flame-thrower tanks, 21 assault guns. Corps artillery: 98 light and 35 medium howitzers, 18
  10-cm guns, 110 anti-tank guns.

## 5. November: Bernhardt line, Mignano, the Sangro

West:
- About 5 Nov (Molony V p.448–449): 305 ID on the left with regimental areas Alfedena, Cerasuolo, Filignano–Montaquila; 3 PG
  left-centre in the triangle Mt Rotondo – Mt Cesima – Mignano, reinforced by III/6 FJ, I/2 HG, 9 PG Regt of 26 Pz (6 Nov)
  and almost all of 26 Pz (8 Nov); 15 PG right-centre with 129 PG in line and 104 PG in reserve at Pontecorvo/Pignataro;
  94 ID on the right; HG refitting at Frosinone except II/1 HG, I/2 HG and the divisional artillery. XIV Pz Corps on 2 Nov:
  173 field guns, 48 assault guns, four werfer batteries, 134 7.5-cm anti-tank guns (p.449). Blumenson instead lists 305
  ID under LXXVI Pz Corps in early November (p.224); Molony is followed here.
- First Camino battle 6–10 Nov: II/104 PG reinforced 7 Nov and I/104 on 9 Nov (Molony V p.452). CMH 100-8 (p.98 or just after) names a regiment printed
  "ngth" in the OCR (probably 129th) as the main defender against 201 Gds Bde. 14 Pz Corps infantry fighting strength 6,354 on 10 Nov (Molony V p.453 fn).
- 10 Nov: left withdrawn to Colle Rotondo – Lagone – Cerasuolo – Mt La Rocca (p.454). 11 Nov: 29 PG ordered from south of
  Rome to relieve 3 PG; its two leading bns counter-attacked near Vallecupa on 13 Nov and failed (p.454). Lemelsen's
  regrouping about 10 Nov gave LXXVI Pz Corps only 1 FJ, 16 Pz (earmarked for Russia) and 65 ID (Blumenson p.233).
- Gustav: work from 10 Nov; G1 on the Garigliano to Mt Valle Martina and the Liri; Gustav from there past Cassino to S.
  Biagio; G4 on to Alfedena; the whole called Gustav by 14 Nov; rear switch Fondi – Pontecorvo – Aquino – Mt Cairo fixed by
  AOK 10 on 26 Nov; Monte Cassino added 29 Nov (Molony V p.430, 479–480).

East:
- Advanced Sangro line Mozzagrogna – Tornareccio – Roccascalegna – Torricella – Gamberale – Castel di Sangro; main
  position ("Siegfried" to LXXVI Pz Corps) north of the Sangro from the sea to Pescocostanzo, Alfedena and Castelnuovo
  a Volturno (Molony V p.457), the forward edge of the Bernhardt line, main works on the Li Colli ridge (p.484).
- 16 Pz still had part of 64 PG at Perano on 17 Nov (Molony V p.486); its withdrawal (to Russia) began about 13 Nov
  (p.478); Blumenson has it "in close support, awaiting transfer" in mid-Nov (p.258). Wikipedia: back to the Eastern Front
  in November 1943; Oberst Back in command from 1 Nov (Molony's table prints "Sieckenius, then Balck", p.478).
- Before the main attack: 65 ID from the coast road to just west of Guardiagrele; 361 PG (90 PG) behind at Villa Grande,
  Miglianico and S. Giovanni Teatino (Molony V p.484). 65 ID: formed in Holland in 1942 of young troops; artillery the
  equivalent of seven troops; 52 7.5-cm anti-tank guns (p.484).
- 26 Pz back to LXXVI Pz Corps from about 26 Nov (Molony V p.477); 90 PG ordered to the Adriatic 21 Nov (Blumenson p.258),
  arriving 29 Nov – 3 Dec (Molony V p.478).
- Sangro battle 27 Nov – 1 Dec (Molony V p.489–491): Mozzagrogna taken night 27/28 Nov; a company of 26 Pz's tank regiment
  (5 Pz IV, 5 flame-throwers, 6 Italian SP guns) counter-attacked at dawn 28 Nov; 29 Nov 65 ID lost Mozzagrogna, S. Maria and
  the high ground to the NZ Div; II/145 written off; 67 PG (26 Pz) could not move by day; von Ziehlberg lost an arm on 28
  Nov (p.493; Wikipedia says left arm); 30 Nov Fossacesia lost; von Usedom's reserve (two bns 67 PG, parts of 361 PG and
  II/26 Pz) at Arielli – Miglianico; Lemelsen: all of 145 Gren Regt a write-off, 361 PG's two bns down to one. Herr then
  delayed on S. Vito – Lanciano – Castelfrentano while preparing Ortona – Orsogna – Melone – Guardiagrele; Kesselring sent
  the rest of 90 PG from Ancona to Pescara and Herr brought 4 FJ Regt over from his right wing (p.491). Westphal: 65 ID "to
  all intents and purposes no longer existed" (Blumenson p.258).
- LXXVI Pz Corps casualties in November: 345 killed, 788 wounded, 1,379 missing (2,512); 21–30 Nov 1,859 (Molony V p.491 fn).

## 6. December: Camino, San Pietro, Moro, Orsogna, Ortona

West:
- 9 Dec fighting strengths (Molony V p.515–516): 305 ID 6,700 (Isernia/Colli approaches to Castel di Sangro, Alfedena,
  Atina); 44 ID newly arrived (Venafro–Filignano road); 29 PG 7,460 (Mt Corno – Mt Sambucaro – S. Pietro – Mt Lungo); 15 PG
  6,660 (Maggiore – La Difensa – Camino); 94 ID 7,430 (Mt Fuga by Castelforte, along the Garigliano to the sea); HG in AOK
  10 reserve, part in the line, part building Gustav. 44 ID arrived 29 Nov – 14 Dec (p.477).
- Second Camino battle 1–9 Dec (Molony V p.517–519): 129 PG in the Calabritto basin; I and III/104 PG and 115 Recce Bn on the
  crests, plus II/104 and II/1 HG on 3 Dec and I/2 HG on 5 Dec; 6 Dec von Senger proposed S. Ambrogio – Cesano – Rocca
  d'Evandro station – east of Mt Lungo; 8 Dec 15 PG authorised to withdraw; 15 PG casualties 974.
- 15 Dec 45 US Div broke into 44 ID at Lagone; 16 Dec Mt Lungo fell; Lemelsen abandoned the Bernhardt sector of 44 ID and
  29 PG for Taverna – S. Vittore – Viticuso – Monna Casale, 305 ID back to Monna Casale – Mainarde; S. Pietro fell 17 Dec
  (Molony V p.519–520). 5 Mtn Div (from Finland, leading troops in Rome 19 Dec) sent to von Senger's northern flank; 29 PG
  began to leave the line only at the end of the year (p.519–520). Korpsgruppe Hauck (one division and an alpine battalion)
  was put between the corps in mid-December (p.502).

East:
- 2 Dec: 26 Pz's Captain Klemme took over Orsogna with 26 Recce Bn, I/9 PG and II/1 FJ (Molony V p.496). 7 Dec on the
  Orsogna ridge: II/9 PG, II/146 Gren, 6 and 8 Coys 26 Pz Regt, part of III/4 FJ (p.498 fn). By 15 Dec four bns held
  Orsogna – Poggiofiorito – Arielli; II/9 PG grievously mauled 15 Dec (p.499).
- 90 PG relieved 65 ID on the coast by 3 Dec (decision of 30 Nov); boundary with 26 Pz through Frisa; 65 ID to a narrow
  sector behind Casoli–Orsogna; 90 PG = 200 PG (three bns), 361 PG, 190 Pz Abt (assault guns, Pz III and IV); Baade
  replaced Lungershausen on 12 Dec (Molony V p.500 and fn). 200 PG and a company of 26 Pz tanks faced the Canadians on the
  Moro and counter-attacked on 6 Dec (p.501).
- 15 Dec: whole 1 FJ Div to the coastal sector (II and III/3 FJ and II/1 FJ at Ortona by 11 Dec); 16 Dec AOK 10 sanctioned a
  step-by-step withdrawal to Pretoro – Villamagna – Torre di Foro (Molony V p.502–503). The Gully, 11–19 Dec: Heilmann with
  II and III/3 FJ, II/1 FJ, the battered 200 PG, a bn of 361 PG, 90 Engineer Bn, tanks of 190 Pz Abt and 26 Pz Regt (p.503).
- Ortona, 21–28 Dec: II/3, III/3 and II/4 FJ (Molony V p.507). Villa Grande – S. Nicola – S. Tommaso: I/3, II/1, III/3 FJ
  and two bns of 4 FJ (p.506; III/3 appears in both lists). 24 Dec AOK 10 considered Torre Mucchia – Villa Grande – Villa S.
  Tommaso; 27 Dec Lemelsen approved leaving Ortona; Herr pulled 90 PG and 1 FJ back to the Tesoro stream (W of Villa Grande –
  Torre Mucchia) while holding Crecchio – Orsogna (p.506).
- Wikipedia (Moro River campaign) puts 1 FJ on the coast, 90 PG to its right, 26 Pz inland to Orsogna, then 65 ID; it also
  mentions 5 Mountain Div elements with 65 ID, which conflicts with Molony (5 Mtn went to XIV Pz Corps) and is not used.
- 334 ID began to arrive about 18 Dec (Molony V p.478); Wikipedia: it relieved 65 ID in the last days of 1943, early 1944
  between Orsogna and Guardiagrele.
- Casualties: LXXVI Pz Corps 1–10 Dec 2,051; AOK 10 in December 13,362 (Molony V p.509 fn).

## Gaps and conflicts

- Nicholson and Phillips could not be read; Canadian/NZ-side detail of German units at the Moro, the Gully, Ortona and
  Orsogna rests on Molony alone. No German divisional histories were available.
- No source found for: a Tiger company in AOK 10 in 1943 (Wikipedia's 44 ID article puts Tigergruppe Meyer, 8 Tigers, with
  44 ID in northern Italy Aug–Nov 1943; Molony's Tigers of Pz Abt 508 come in 1944); LXXVI Pz Corps werfer or StuG units;
  the location of 56 Werfer Regt and 242 StuG Abt; the counter "Reggio".
- Italian units in German service: none found for AOK 10 in this period; only six Italian SP guns with 26 Pz (Molony V p.489).
- Dates differ: 16 Pz's Termoli move (1, 2 or 4 Oct); 26 Pz's return west (6/8 Nov Molony, 9 Nov CMH 100-8); 305 ID's corps
  in early Nov (Molony XIV, Blumenson LXXVI).
- OCR problems: 26 Pz fighting strength on 4 Nov ("53390"); the sideways diagram of the Volturno front (Molony V p.442); the
  footnote on the four formations sent to the Adriatic in Oct/early Nov is partly lost (only "...Ravenna; Sturmbrigade
  Reichsführer S.S., Teramo; 65th Division, Pescara area" survives, p.437).
- Positions are given for the dates the sources name; in between, units should be interpolated along the lines above.
  Several battalion positions are inferred from the regiment or group and are flagged `uncertain` in the JSON.
- Coordinates: places with no Wikipedia coordinates (Chiunzi pass, Villa Grande, Torre Mucchia, San Leonardo, Melone,
  Cerasuolo, Lagone, Taverna, Monna Casale, Mt Valle Martina, Monte Camino, Monte Sammucro, Monte Lungo, Frisa, Pratella)
  use a nearby comune as a stated proxy; Polla, Atina and Aquino use the repo's `tools/grand/towns.py`; Colle Rotondo is unplaced.
